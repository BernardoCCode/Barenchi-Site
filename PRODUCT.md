# PRODUCT.md

## Product
Barenchi builds digital products around real business problems: landing pages, custom systems, ERP/CRM, ecommerce ecosystems, internal tools, integrations, and automation. Based in Brazil.

## Users
Founders and operators who need software tailored to how their company actually works — not a generic SaaS template.

## Mechanism
Idea → software → system → business. Barenchi designs and ships the product, not just the website.

## Voice
Confident, precise, short. Portuguese-Brazilian company, English landing copy as specified.

## Platform
web

## Stack
React 19, Vite, TypeScript, motion/react, lucide-react, GSAP. Plain CSS. Instrument Sans + Instrument Serif. No Tailwind. No UI kit.

## Constraints
- White / off-white is the primary identity (~90–95%). Brown (`#6B4A35`) is a 5–10% accent only — never section backgrounds
- The hero uses the supplied white robot software film as its only rich media; clickable moments connect the scene to real Barenchi capabilities
- Do not bake typography, logos, or invented metrics into hero media
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Mobile-first, 768px breakpoint
